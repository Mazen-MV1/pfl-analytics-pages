Privacy Policy

Pocket Frame Lab Analytics

Last updated: November 27, 2026

1. Application Purpose

Pocket Frame Lab Analytics is a personal automation tool designed to monitor and analyze the performance of the Pocket Frame Lab YouTube channel. It is intended exclusively for the channel owner's personal use.

2. Data Accessed

The application uses official Google and YouTube APIs to access:

  • Channel and video performance statistics.
  • Views, watch time and audience engagement.
  • Audience geography and traffic sources.
  • Audience retention and thumbnail impressions.
  • Video titles, publication dates and durations.
  • Subscriber growth statistics.

YouTube permissions are read-only. The application does not publish, modify or delete YouTube content.

3. Data Storage

Retrieved analytics are written to a private Google spreadsheet controlled by the channel owner. The spreadsheet is not publicly accessible.

4. Data Usage and Sharing

Analytics data is used for channel performance monitoring, content planning and performance reviews.

Instinct, an authorized third-party AI assistant, accesses the spreadsheet on behalf of the channel owner to provide analytics and content planning recommendations.

The application does not sell analytics data or make it publicly available. Instinct's processing of information is subject to its own applicable privacy policies and terms.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Analytics remain in the private spreadsheet until deleted by the channel owner. The owner may delete stored information or disconnect authorized services at any time.

6. Revoking Access

The channel owner may revoke Google account permissions at any time through Google Account Permissions. Revoking access prevents future authorized data retrieval.

7. Google API Services User Data Policy

The application is intended to comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Policy Updates

This privacy policy may be updated to reflect changes in the application's functionality or data handling practices.
